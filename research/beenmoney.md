### ⬩ overview

**beenmoney** is an ultra-minimalist, client-side budgeting tool packaged entirely within a single HTML file. designed with a dark, modern aesthetic, it effortlessly tracks your income and expenses while living completely in your browser.

no databases. no backend. no friction.

### ⬩ features

- **✧ pure minimalism:** everything resides in one standalone .html file.
- **✧ dark aesthetic:** deeply saturated #1a202c backgrounds tailored for late-night finance management.
- **✧ real-time balance:** instant calculations for current balance and monthly expenses.
- **✧ zero footprint:** all data exists locally.
- **✧ pdf export:** cleanly generate structured reports utilizing jspdf.

### ⬩ setup

there's no 
pm install. there's no build step.

1. download Been's Money: Simple Budgeting App.html
2. open it in your favorite browser.
3. take control of your finances.

### ⬩ tech stack

- **structure:** standard html5
- **styling:** 	ailwindcss (via cdn)
- **logic:** vanilla javascript
- **export:** jspdf & jspdf-autotable
- **typography:** inter (google fonts)
